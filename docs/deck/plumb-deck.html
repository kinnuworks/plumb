The vault

PlumbBidVault

  1. Deposit mUSDC, get shares. Capped at 5,000 mUSDC.
  2. Half goes into a band on the live price; the vault swaps just enough into MUSD, never paying more than the TWAP plus one tick slot or more than $1.
  3. The rest is a bid from the band down to about $0.98. Both are staked in the pool's gauge.
  4. MUSD the bid buys earns in sMUSD and is offered back only at $1 or more.
  5. Exits are pro rata and in kind, any time. No owner, no admin.

Who pays, who earns

  • The pool's gauge pays about 87,350 MEZO a day to liquidity at the price. Today a handful of positions collect it.
  • A depositor's share: $20,000 earned 3,976 MEZO in a quiet day after fees, about $11.90 at today's MEZO price (0.0030 MUSD). It falls as more liquidity joins.
  • The vault keeps 10% of MEZO, posts 10% back to the gauge's voters, tips 1% to whoever rebalances (under $0.20 of gas).
  • The risk: MUSD bought below $1, held until it sells at $1 or more.
