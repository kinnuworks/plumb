The vault

PlumbBidVault

  1. Deposit mUSDC, get shares.
  2. The vault bids from one tick under the market to 2% deeper, staked in the pool's gauge.
  3. MUSD it buys earns in sMUSD while MUSD is under $1.
  4. Near $1 it is offered back, never below $1.
  5. MEZO streams to depositors. 10% is posted back to the same gauge to pull veBTC votes.
  6. Exits are pro rata and in kind, any time.

sMUSD strategy

PlumbAskStrategy

  • 7.38M MUSD sits in sMUSD's IdleStrategy, earning nothing.
  • One setStrategy call: 95% stays idle, 5% becomes a standing offer at $1.001 and up.
  • On the fork, the real owner switches all 7,382,555 MUSD over. A full cycle returns 164 MUSD to savers, and switching back returns every token.
  • 90% of savers can leave at once with the order filled. Sandwiched buybacks are refused.
7 / 10

Safety

  • No owner, no admin, no upgrades, no signatures or meta-transactions.
  • Spot must agree with the TWAP before any liquidity moves or any deposit is priced.
  • Floor at $0.96 and a 60% inventory cap stop the vault buying all the way down.
  • 48 tests and 10 invariants against real mainnet state. The invariants found a real share-pricing bug; blind review found two more. All fixed, each with a test.
