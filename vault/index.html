Plumb vault

You deposit mUSDC. Half of it buys and sells MUSD right at today's price, which is where the pool pays its MEZO rewards. The other half waits as an order to buy MUSD if the price drops, down to about $0.98. MUSD the vault buys below $1 earns savings interest until it can be sold at $1 or more. Withdraw any time: you get your share of whatever the vault holds, mUSDC and MUSD.
