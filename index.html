A standing bid under MUSD

Nearly every buyer of MUSD on Mezo is a handful of positions parked at one price, about $0.994, in the main MUSD/mUSDC pool. Below that price almost nobody is bidding, so a large sale runs out of buyers. Plumb is a vault that turns your mUSDC into more buyers at that price and below it, and pays you the pool's MEZO rewards for it (the pool pays about 87,350 MEZO a day to liquidity at the current price).

  • Deposits are capped at 5,000 mUSDC. Withdraw any time; you get your share of each token back.
  • The risk: MUSD the vault buys below $1 is held until it can be sold at $1 or more, so you carry that MUSD if the price stays low.
  • No owner, no admin keys. Unaudited; a fresh review's findings are fixed and listed in the repo.

When those positions leave, the buy side goes with them

From late August to mid October a 100,000 MUSD sale paid about $99,000. Then the bid side thinned: replayed at a block on Oct 22, selling 10,000 MUSD would have paid $11. Depth came back once the concentrated positions returned.
